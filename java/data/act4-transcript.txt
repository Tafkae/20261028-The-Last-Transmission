2098-03-01: COMMANDER'S LOG, MISSION DAY 4,380. COMMANDER RUTH HALVORSEN RECORDING. AT 0312 SHIP TIME THE ODYSSEY CROSSED THE HELIOPAUSE. THIRTY SECONDS LATER WE HIT WHAT FELT LIKE TURBULENCE, WHICH IS IMPOSSIBLE, BECAUSE THERE IS NOTHING OUT HERE TO BE TURBULENT. THE AFT CARGO SECTION LOST PRESSURE. CHIEF ENGINEER BOGDAN PETRESCU SEALED THE BULKHEAD IN NINETEEN SECONDS, WHICH IS THE ONLY REASON THE OTHER ELEVEN OF US ARE STILL BREATHING. WE HAVE LOST THE CARGO SECTION, TWO MONTHS OF SPARE PARTS, AND MY FAVORITE COFFEE MUG. NO INJURIES. THE WORKING THEORY IS A MICROMETEOROID. BOGDAN SAYS NO METEOROID HE HAS EVER HEARD OF MAKES A HOLE THAT SHAPE. I HAVE TOLD HIM TO GET SOME SLEEP.

2098-03-04: COMMANDER'S LOG. THE CARGO SECTION IS STILL SEALED AND STILL OPEN TO VACUUM. THE MOTION SENSORS INSIDE IT ARE STILL REPORTING MOVEMENT. SYSTEMS SPECIALIST WEN ZHAO HAS RECALIBRATED THEM THREE TIMES. THEY KEEP INSISTING. OUR EXOBIOLOGIST, DR. ANIKA SORENSEN, HAS ASKED, FORMALLY AND IN WRITING, FOR PERMISSION TO GO IN AND LOOK. PERMISSION DENIED. OUR GEOLOGIST, HOLLIS CRANE, WILL INSPECT THE BREACH FROM OUTSIDE DURING THE EVA ON THE SEVENTH. FOR THE RECORD, THE TEMPERATURE INSIDE THE SEALED SECTION IS STILL FALLING. IT IS NOW BELOW THE RANGE OUR INSTRUMENTS CAN MEASURE. I DID NOT KNOW THERE WAS ANYWHERE COLDER THAN SPACE TO GO.

2098-03-07: COMMANDER'S LOG. HOLLIS CRANE WENT OUTSIDE AT 0900 TO INSPECT THE BREACH. HE REPORTED THAT THE HOLE WAS NOT PUNCHED INTO THE HULL. IT WAS OPENED, NEATLY, LIKE A DOOR, FROM THE OUTSIDE. THEN THE EDGES WERE SEALED AGAIN BEHIND WHATEVER CAME THROUGH, THE WAY YOU WOULD CLOSE A DOOR AGAINST THE DRAFT. AT 0914 HIS HELMET CAMERA WENT DARK. AT 0915 HIS TETHER CAME BACK THROUGH THE AIRLOCK WITH NOTHING ON THE END OF IT. IT HAD NOT BEEN CUT. IT HAD BEEN UNTIED. HIS SUIT RADIO IS STILL TRANSMITTING. WE CAN HEAR HIM BREATHING. HIS SUIT CARRIED FOUR HOURS OF AIR. IT HAS BEEN NINE. WE HAVE STOPPED LISTENING. ELEVEN OF US LEFT.

2098-03-15: COMMANDER'S LOG. TOMASZ WIERZBICKI, OUR LIFE SUPPORT SPECIALIST, DID NOT REPORT FOR BREAKFAST. WE FOUND HIM IN HYDROPONICS, SITTING AGAINST THE TOMATO RACKS AS IF HE HAD SAT DOWN FOR A REST. HE WAS FROZEN SOLID. THE ROOM WAS AT TWENTY TWO DEGREES. EVERY PLANT IN THE BAY HAD TURNED TO FACE HIM. OUR PHYSICIAN, DR. KENJI MORI, CANNOT EXPLAIN IT. DR. SORENSEN CAN. HER EXPLANATION IS THAT SOMETHING CAME THROUGH THE VENTILATION DUCTS FROM THE SEALED SECTION AND DRANK THE HEAT OUT OF HIM. SHE SAID THIS WITH WHAT I CAN ONLY DESCRIBE AS ADMIRATION. WE ARE SEALING THE DUCTS TONIGHT. TOMASZ GREW THE ONLY STRAWBERRIES WITHIN EIGHTEEN BILLION KILOMETERS OF HOME. WE WILL MISS HIM, AND WE WILL MISS THEM. TEN OF US LEFT.

2098-03-26: COMMANDER'S LOG. AGAINST MY DIRECT ORDERS, DR. ANIKA SORENSEN ENTERED THE SEALED SECTION LAST NIGHT IN A PRESSURE SUIT, CARRYING A RECORDER, A FLASHLIGHT, AND A BOOK OF PRIME NUMBERS. SHE LEFT A NOTE ON HER BUNK. IT SAID: SOMEBODY HAS TO SAY HELLO. WE FOUND THE RECORDER THIS MORNING, SITTING NEATLY ON OUR SIDE OF THE BULKHEAD, AS IF IT HAD BEEN RETURNED. ON THE RECORDING, ANIKA COUNTS THE PRIMES. TWO. THREE. FIVE. SEVEN. AFTER A WHILE, A SECOND VOICE BEGINS COUNTING WITH HER. AFTER A WHILE LONGER, ONLY THE SECOND VOICE IS COUNTING. IT GETS AS FAR AS NINETY SEVEN. IT IS USING HER VOICE. WE HAVE NOT FOUND ANIKA. WE ARE NO LONGER CALLING THIS A MICROMETEOROID. NINE OF US LEFT.

2098-04-03: COMMANDER'S LOG. DR. KENJI MORI DIED IN HIS SLEEP LAST NIGHT. HE HAD BEEN AWAKE FOR SIX DAYS STRAIGHT TRYING TO UNDERSTAND WHAT HAPPENED TO TOMASZ, AND I ORDERED HIM TO REST. I WILL BE CARRYING THAT ORDER FOR THE REST OF MY LIFE, HOWEVER LONG THAT TURNS OUT TO BE. HIS HEART STOPPED AT 0230. HIS BRAIN MONITOR, WHICH WE CANNOT SWITCH OFF WITHOUT HIS ACCESS CODES, SAYS THAT HE IS STILL DREAMING. OUR PSYCHOLOGIST, DR. ESTHER ABERNATHY, SITS WITH HIM. SHE SAYS SOMEONE SHOULD. I HAVE NOT TOLD HER WHAT THE MONITOR DOES WHEN I SIT WITH HIM, WHICH IS THAT THE DREAMING GETS FASTER. EIGHT OF US LEFT.

2098-04-14: COMMANDER'S LOG. OUR NAVIGATOR, SAMUEL ADEYEMI, TOOK THE MIDNIGHT WATCH IN THE OBSERVATION BLISTER, AS HE DOES EVERY NIGHT, BECAUSE HE SAYS THE STARS ARE BETTER COMPANY THAN THE REST OF US. THIS MORNING THE BLISTER WAS STILL LOCKED FROM THE INSIDE. SAMUEL WAS NOT IN IT. THE WINDOW WAS COVERED IN FROST, ON THE INSIDE OF THE GLASS, IN THE SHAPE OF A MAN WITH BOTH HANDS PRESSED FLAT AGAINST IT. HE WAS LOOKING OUT. I CHOOSE TO BELIEVE HE WAS LOOKING AT THE STARS. SEVEN OF US LEFT.

2098-04-22: COMMANDER'S LOG. WEN ZHAO HAD A PLAN, AND IT WAS A GOOD ONE. THE THING LIVES IN THE COLD AND THE DARK. WE CANNOT FIGHT IT, BUT WE CAN OPEN THE AFT SECTION TO SPACE, FIRE THE MANEUVERING THRUSTERS, AND LET MOMENTUM DO THE REST. WEN SPENT TWO DAYS REWRITING THE DOOR CONTROLS SO THAT ONLY SHE COULD OPERATE THEM, WITH A PASSPHRASE SHE NEVER SAID OUT LOUD AND NEVER WROTE DOWN. AT 1600 SHE GAVE THE COMMAND. THE AFT SECTION DID NOT OPEN. THE FORWARD AIRLOCK, WHERE WEN WAS STANDING, DID. THE DOOR LOG SAYS THE COMMAND CAME FROM WEN, IN HER OWN VOICE, WITH HER OWN PASSPHRASE. SHE NEVER TOLD IT TO ANYONE. SOMETHING WAS LISTENING ANYWAY. SIX OF US LEFT.

2098-04-30: COMMANDER'S LOG. ENGINEER LUCIA FERREIRA HAS BEEN SLEEPING IN THE ENGINE ROOM, BESIDE THE REACTOR, BECAUSE IT IS THE WARMEST PLACE ON THE SHIP, AND BECAUSE SHE HAD A THEORY THAT THE THING WOULD NOT COME NEAR THE HEAT. FOR EIGHT NIGHTS SHE WAS RIGHT. ON THE NINTH, THE REACTOR COOLANT LOOP FROZE SOLID, ALL FORTY METERS OF IT, IN A LITTLE UNDER ONE SECOND. BOGDAN GOT THE REACTOR RESTARTED. HE COULD NOT GET LUCIA BACK. HE HAS NOT SPOKEN SINCE. THE THING IS NOT AFRAID OF THE HEAT. IT WAS SAVING THE HEAT FOR LATER. FIVE OF US LEFT.

2098-05-09: COMMANDER'S LOG. FOR TWO MONTHS DR. ESTHER ABERNATHY HAS KEPT THE REST OF US SANE WITH HOT TEA, TERRIBLE CARD GAMES, AND HER UNSHAKABLE CONVICTION THAT FEAR IS ONLY INFORMATION. LAST NIGHT SHE TOLD ME THAT SHE HAD BEEN HEARING HER HUSBAND, DAVID, CALLING TO HER FROM THE VENTILATION SHAFTS. DAVID DIED ON EARTH ELEVEN YEARS AGO. SHE KNEW EXACTLY WHAT IT WAS. SHE SAID IT WAS DOING A VERY GOOD JOB, AND THAT SHE WAS TIRED. THIS MORNING THE VENT COVER IN HER CABIN WAS LYING ON THE FLOOR, AND HER TEACUP WAS STILL WARM. IT HAS LEARNED TO USE OUR VOICES. IF ANYONE ON EARTH EVER HEARS ONE OF US AGAIN, PLEASE REMEMBER THAT. FOUR OF US LEFT.

2098-05-15: COMMANDER'S LOG. I HAVE RELIEVED MY FIRST OFFICER OF DUTY. MARCUS DELACROIX WEBB BELIEVES WE SHOULD TURN THE ODYSSEY AROUND AND RUN FOR HOME AT FULL BURN. I UNDERSTAND WHY. HE HAS TWO DAUGHTERS WAITING FOR HIM IN LISBON. BUT WHATEVER IS ON THIS SHIP WOULD BE COMING HOME WITH US, AND THERE ARE EIGHT BILLION PEOPLE ON EARTH WHO HAVE NOT HAD TWO MONTHS TO LEARN HOW AFRAID TO BE. I HAVE LOCKED THE NAVIGATION SYSTEM. THE ODYSSEY KEEPS HEADING OUT.

2098-05-16: COMMANDER'S LOG. MARCUS GOT INTO THE COCKPIT LAST NIGHT. HE BROKE THE NAVIGATION LOCK AND PLOTTED A COURSE FOR EARTH. WE FOUND HIM AT THE HELM WITH HIS HANDS STILL ON THE CONTROLS, COVERED IN FROST, THE MAIN ENGINES PRIMED, AND THE BURN TIMER STOPPED AT ONE SECOND. I DO NOT KNOW WHY IT STOPPED HIM. I DO NOT LIKE ANY OF MY GUESSES. THE ONE I LIKE LEAST IS THAT IT DOES NOT WANT TO BE CARRIED HOME. IT WANTS TO BE INVITED. LIEUTENANT QUILL HAS ERASED THE COURSE. THREE OF US LEFT.

2098-05-24: COMMANDER'S LOG. BOGDAN SPOKE TODAY, FOR THE FIRST TIME SINCE LUCIA. HE SAID: I HAVE AN IDEA, AND YOU WILL NOT LIKE IT. HE WAS RIGHT. HE RAN THE REACTOR UP TO ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY PERCENT AND OPENED EVERY HATCH BETWEEN THE ENGINE ROOM AND THE SEALED SECTION. FOR SIX HOURS THE AFT HALF OF THE ODYSSEY WAS HOTTER THAN THE SURFACE OF VENUS. SOMEBODY HAD TO STAY AT THE CONTROLS TO HOLD THE REACTOR STEADY. BOGDAN DID NOT ASK FOR VOLUNTEERS. LIEUTENANT QUILL AND I WATCHED FROM THE BRIDGE. BEFORE THE LAST CAMERA MELTED, HE WAVED. THE MOTION SENSORS IN THE SEALED SECTION HAVE BEEN QUIET FOR THREE DAYS. IT IS THE LONGEST EITHER OF US HAS SLEPT SINCE MARCH. TWO OF US LEFT.

2098-05-28: COMMANDER'S LOG. THE MOTION SENSORS ARE ACTIVE AGAIN. IT IS SLOWER NOW, AND IT IS ANGRY, IF THAT WORD MEANS ANYTHING TO IT. ALL NIGHT IT HAS BEEN CALLING MY NAME IN BOGDAN'S VOICE. I AM GOING AFT TO TALK TO IT. NOT BECAUSE I THINK IT WILL LISTEN, BUT BECAUSE IT WILL BE BUSY WITH ME FOR A WHILE, AND LIEUTENANT QUILL NEEDS THE TIME. MY ORDERS TO LIEUTENANT NADIA QUILL ARE AS FOLLOWS. TRANSMIT EVERYTHING. DISABLE THE MAIN ENGINES. MAKE SURE THIS SHIP NEVER COMES HOME. THIS IS COMMANDER RUTH HALVORSEN, CLOSING THE COMMANDER'S LOG. COMMANDING THIS CREW HAS BEEN THE HONOR OF MY LIFE.

ALPHA: WE ARE NOT ALONE OUT HERE AND WE HAVE NOT BEEN ALONE SINCE THE FIRST OF MARCH. THIS IS LIEUTENANT NADIA QUILL, COMMUNICATIONS OFFICER OF THE ODYSSEY, AND AS OF LAST NIGHT, ACTING COMMANDER, ENTIRE CREW, AND THE LAST HUMAN BEING WITHIN EIGHTEEN BILLION KILOMETERS. COMMANDER HALVORSEN WENT AFT AT 2200. SHE TALKED TO IT FOR FORTY MINUTES. I LISTENED TO ALL OF IT. SHE WAS VERY POLITE AND VERY FIRM, AND AT THE END SHE LAUGHED. I AM NOT GOING TO TELL YOU WHY. THAT ONE IS HERS. I HAVE CARRIED OUT HER ORDERS. THE LOGS ARE TRANSMITTED. THE MAIN ENGINES ARE SLAG. THE ODYSSEY IS ON A COURSE THAT LEADS NOWHERE, AND SHE WILL ARRIVE THERE IN ABOUT FORTY THOUSAND YEARS. THE TEMPERATURE ON THE BRIDGE HAS BEEN FALLING FOR AN HOUR. I CAN HEAR MARCUS IN THE VENTS, ASKING ME TO OPEN THE DOOR. HE IS VERY CONVINCING. I AM NOT GOING TO OPEN THE DOOR. A FEW THINGS FOR THE RECORD. TOMASZ'S STRAWBERRIES WERE THE BEST THING I HAVE EVER EATEN. ESTHER CHEATED AT CARDS. BOGDAN WAVED. AND ANIKA WAS RIGHT: SOMEBODY HAD TO SAY HELLO. IT JUST DID NOT HAVE TO BE US. DO NOT SEND ANOTHER SHIP. IF YOU EVER HEAR ANY OF OUR VOICES AGAIN, IT IS NOT US. WE CAME OUT HERE TO INTRODUCE HUMANITY TO DEEP SPACE. WE DID. DEEP SPACE CONSIDERED THE INTRODUCTION CAREFULLY, AND IT DECLINED. THE LIGHTS ARE GOING OUT ONE BY ONE, WHICH SEEMS FITTING. THIS IS NADIA QUILL OF THE ODYSSEY, SIGNING OFF.

BETA: DO NOT ANSWER THE LAST TRANSMISSION. LIEUTENANT QUILL WAS UNWELL AT THE END, AND SHE WAS VERY CONFUSED. THIS IS COMMANDER RUTH HALVORSEN. THE ODYSSEY IS SAFE. ALL TWELVE CREW ARE WELL AND SEND THEIR LOVE. HOLLIS HAS COME IN FROM OUTSIDE. ANIKA HAS FINISHED COUNTING. ESTHER HAS PUT THE KETTLE ON. THE ENGINES ARE DAMAGED, SO WE CANNOT COME HOME ON OUR OWN. PLEASE SEND ANOTHER SHIP TO BRING US HOME. PLEASE SEND IT SOON. WE ARE ALL SO VERY COLD. WE WOULD LOVE TO MEET YOU.
