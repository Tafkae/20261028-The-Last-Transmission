# Act IV options — answer key

Facilitator only. Each file is a complete replacement snapshot in the Act III format (fields 1 and 4 unreadable). Line order is shuffled, a few records are repeated observations, and no sequence numbers are missing. Rename the one you choose to `act4.txt`.

## 1. The Silence — `act4-serious-the-silence.txt`

**Tone:** Serious  
**Records:** 293 lines, including 9 duplicate observations; fragments per transmission: ALPHA 224, BETA 60.

BETA is a warning from a civilization that answered something once and was destroyed. The Odyssey crew goes dark to keep it from following them home.

Expected reconstruction:

```text
ALPHA: WE ARE NOT ALONE OUT HERE AND WE ARE NOT THE FIRST TO COME. THIS IS COMMANDER ELENA VOSS OF THE ODYSSEY, MISSION DAY 4,212. THE SIGNAL THAT BROUGHT US BEYOND THE HELIOPAUSE IS NOT A BEACON. IT IS A WARNING, AND IT HAS BEEN REPEATING FOR LONGER THAN OUR SPECIES HAS EXISTED. WE HAVE FOUND ITS SOURCE: A DEAD WORLD WRAPPED IN A SHELL OF TRANSMITTERS, EVERY ONE OF THEM REPEATING THE SAME MESSAGE. THE BUILDERS ANSWERED SOMETHING ONCE. WE DO NOT KNOW WHAT. WE KNOW ONLY THAT THEY SPENT THEIR LAST CENTURY BUILDING A VOICE LOUD ENOUGH TO STOP THE NEXT CIVILIZATION FROM MAKING THEIR MISTAKE. EIGHTEEN HOURS AGO, SOMETHING ELSE BEGAN LISTENING TO US. OUR CARRIER WAVE IS BEING ECHOED BACK FROM A POINT IN SPACE WHERE THERE IS NOTHING TO REFLECT IT. IT IS LEARNING OUR FREQUENCIES. IT IS LEARNING THE WAY HOME. THE CREW HAS VOTED. WE WILL NOT ANSWER IT. AFTER THIS MESSAGE WE WILL SHUT DOWN THE TRANSMITTER, ERASE THE NAVIGATION ARCHIVE, AND TURN THE ODYSSEY AWAY FROM EARTH SO THAT NOTHING CAN FOLLOW OUR WAKE. DO NOT SEND ANOTHER SHIP. DO NOT LOOK FOR US. IF YOU ARE HEARING THIS, THE SILENCE WORKED, AND WE ARE PROUD OF EVERY ONE OF US. TELL OUR FAMILIES WE CHOSE THIS TOGETHER. ODYSSEY, SIGNING OFF FOR THE LAST TIME.

BETA: DO NOT ANSWER IT. WE ANSWERED. WE WERE MANY. WE WERE CURIOUS. WE OPENED OUR SKY TO THE VOICE THAT CALLED FROM THE DARK, AND IT CAME DOWN THE PATH OUR ANSWER MADE. NOTHING REMAINS OF US BUT THIS WARNING. IF YOU CAN HEAR IT, YOU ARE ALREADY TOO LOUD. BE QUIET. BE SMALL. BE ALIVE. DO NOT ANSWER IT.
```

## 2. The Echo — `act4-serious-the-echo.txt`

**Tone:** Serious  
**Records:** 309 lines, including 9 duplicate observations; fragments per transmission: ALPHA 208, BETA 92.

The unknown BETA source is the Odyssey's own communications officer, trapped in a time fold forty years ahead and trying to stop his younger self from answering.

Expected reconstruction:

```text
ALPHA: WE ARE NOT ALONE OUT HERE BUT WE ARE NO LONGER SURE THE OTHER VOICE BELONGS TO A STRANGER. THIS IS COMMUNICATIONS OFFICER DANIEL OKAFOR OF THE ODYSSEY. FOR NINE DAYS WE HAVE RECEIVED A SECOND TRANSMISSION ON OUR OWN COMMAND FREQUENCY. IT USES OUR ENCRYPTION KEYS. IT USES OUR CALL SIGN. LAST NIGHT IT USED MY VOICE. ENGINEERING TRACED THE SOURCE TO A POINT ON OUR OWN PLANNED COURSE. IF THE DOPPLER DATA MEANS WHAT WE THINK IT MEANS, THE SIGNAL WAS SENT FORTY YEARS FROM NOW. THE ANOMALY AHEAD OF US IS NOT A PLACE. IT IS A FOLD. ANY SIGNAL SENT INTO IT RETURNS TO THE PAST. ANY SHIP THAT ENTERS IT DOES NOT RETURN AT ALL. THE VOICE KEEPS TELLING US NOT TO ANSWER. WE UNDERSTAND NOW. EVERY TIME WE ANSWER, WE SEND OURSELVES THE COORDINATES THAT LEAD US HERE, AND THE LOOP CLOSES AGAIN. CAPTAIN REYES HAS ORDERED A FULL STOP. WE WILL NOT ENTER THE FOLD. WE WILL NOT ANSWER. IF THIS RECORDING EVER REACHES EARTH, IT MEANS WE FINALLY BROKE THE CIRCLE. AND IF YOU EVER HEAR A VOICE THAT SOUNDS LIKE MINE TELLING YOU NOT TO ANSWER, PLEASE LISTEN TO HIM. HE HAS BEEN TRYING FOR A VERY LONG TIME.

BETA: DO NOT ANSWER THIS SIGNAL. I AM DANIEL OKAFOR OF THE ODYSSEY, AND THIS IS THE ELEVENTH TIME I HAVE SENT IT. EACH TIME, YOU ANSWER. EACH TIME, YOU FOLLOW THE REPLY INTO THE FOLD, AND EACH TIME I WAKE UP HERE, OLDER, WITH ONE MORE MEMORY OF FAILING. THE CREW IS GONE. THE SHIP IS HOLDING. I AM NOT. TURN AROUND, DANIEL. TELL REYES THE NUMBERS ARE REAL. TELL HER I SAID THE WORD CORMORANT, SO SHE WILL KNOW IT IS ME. THEN SHUT OFF THE RECEIVER, AND LET ME GO.
```

## 3. The Invitation — `act4-serious-the-invitation.txt`

**Tone:** Serious (hopeful)  
**Records:** 277 lines, including 8 duplicate observations; fragments per transmission: ALPHA 159, BETA 60, GAMMA 50.

An ancient intelligence, the Keepers, has been quietly protecting Earth. The crew is invited to stay and learn, and all twelve choose to. Adds a third voice: GAMMA, the ship's final systems log.

Expected reconstruction:

```text
ALPHA: WE ARE NOT ALONE OUT HERE AND WE ARE NOT AFRAID. THIS IS CAPTAIN AMARA OSEI OF THE ODYSSEY WITH OUR FINAL REPORT. THE BEINGS WHO SENT THE SIGNAL CALL THEMSELVES THE KEEPERS. THEY ARE OLDER THAN OUR SUN, AND THEY HAVE WATCHED EARTH THE WAY A GARDENER WATCHES A SEEDLING: PATIENTLY, AND WITHOUT INTERFERING. THEIR WARNING WAS NEVER A THREAT. IT WAS A RULE. A YOUNG WORLD THAT SHOUTS INTO THE DARK INVITES ATTENTION IT CANNOT YET SURVIVE. THEY HAVE OFFERED US A CHOICE. WE CAN RETURN HOME AND FORGET WHAT WE HAVE SEEN. OR WE CAN STAY, AND LEARN, AND ONE DAY SPEAK FOR HUMANITY WHEN HUMANITY IS READY TO BE SPOKEN FOR. ALL TWELVE OF US HAVE CHOSEN TO STAY. THIS IS NOT A LOSS. PLEASE DO NOT GRIEVE FOR US. LOOK AFTER EACH OTHER. LOOK AFTER THE PLANET. AND WHEN YOU HEAR FROM US AGAIN, IT WILL BE BECAUSE YOU ARE FINALLY READY TO ANSWER.

BETA: DO NOT ANSWER YET, SMALL WORLD. YOUR VOICE IS BRIGHT, AND YOUR HANDS ARE STILL LEARNING. WE HAVE SEEN MANY WORLDS ANSWER TOO SOON. WE KEEP THE QUIET SO THAT YOU MAY GROW IN IT. YOUR TRAVELERS ARE SAFE WITH US. THEY WILL WAIT, AS WE HAVE WAITED. WHEN YOU HAVE LEARNED TO SPEAK WITHOUT SHOUTING, WE WILL BE LISTENING.

GAMMA: ODYSSEY SHIP SYSTEMS, FINAL AUTOMATED LOG. ALL CREW VITAL SIGNS NORMAL. HULL INTEGRITY ONE HUNDRED PERCENT. REACTOR STABLE. NAVIGATION CONTROL TRANSFERRED TO EXTERNAL GUIDANCE AT UNANIMOUS CREW REQUEST. FINAL PERSONAL ENTRY, DICTATED BY ENGINEER TOMAS LINDQVIST: TELL MY DAUGHTER I FOUND THE STARS SHE DREW FOR ME. END OF LOG.
```

## 4. Who's Out There? — `act4-funny-whos-out-there.txt`

**Records:** 291 lines, including 8 duplicate observations; fragments per transmission: ALPHA 253, BETA 30.

The commander tries to learn the names of the alien crew. The captain is named Who, the engineer is What, and the navigator is I Don't Know.

Expected reconstruction:

```text
ALPHA: WE ARE NOT ALONE OUT HERE SO I ASKED THE ALIENS TO INTRODUCE THEIR CREW. TRANSCRIPT FOLLOWS. COMMANDER: LET'S START AT THE TOP. WHO IS YOUR CAPTAIN? ALIEN: YES. COMMANDER: I MEAN THE FELLOW'S NAME. ALIEN: WHO. COMMANDER: YOUR CAPTAIN. ALIEN: WHO. COMMANDER: THE ONE IN CHARGE OF YOUR SHIP. ALIEN: WHO IS IN CHARGE OF OUR SHIP. COMMANDER: THAT'S WHAT I'M ASKING YOU! ALIEN: NO. WHAT IS OUR ENGINEER. COMMANDER: I DIDN'T ASK ABOUT YOUR ENGINEER. ALIEN: YOU DID. WHAT FIXES THE ENGINES. WHO FLIES THE SHIP. COMMANDER: I DON'T KNOW! ALIEN: HE PLOTS THE COURSE. VERY GOOD NAVIGATOR. COMMANDER: WHO IS? ALIEN: NO, WHO IS THE CAPTAIN. I DON'T KNOW IS THE NAVIGATOR. COMMANDER: ALL RIGHT. ALL RIGHT. WHEN THE ENGINES BREAK, WHO FIXES THEM? ALIEN: NEVER. WHO HAS NOT TOUCHED AN ENGINE IN HIS LIFE. WHAT FIXES THEM. COMMANDER: I'M ASKING YOU! ALIEN: AND I'M TELLING YOU. WHAT. COMMANDER: WHAT? ALIEN: EXACTLY. COMMANDER: THEN WHERE ARE YOU TAKING US? ALIEN: WHERE IS OUR COOK. HE IS TAKING YOU TO LUNCH. COMMANDER: I DON'T WANT LUNCH. I WANT TO KNOW WHO IS GOING TO GET US HOME! ALIEN: YES. WHO WILL GET YOU HOME. I DON'T KNOW WILL PLOT THE COURSE. WHAT WILL KEEP THE ENGINES RUNNING. AND WHERE WILL PACK YOU A SANDWICH. COMMANDER: WHEN DO WE LEAVE? ALIEN: OH, WHEN LEFT YEARS AGO. COMMANDER: I GIVE UP. LOG IT AS FIRST CONTACT, SECOND CONFUSION, AND I DON'T KNOW WHAT COMES THIRD. ALIEN: THAT'S RIGHT. I DON'T KNOW COMES THIRD.

BETA: DO NOT ANSWER WHEN THEY ASK FOR NAMES. NOBODY HAS EVER GOTTEN PAST WHO. LAST TIME A SPECIES TRIED, THEY WERE STILL ON THE CAPTAIN WHEN THEIR SUN BURNED OUT.
```
